Section 1

Move Reservation Policy

Your refundable deposit secures your moving date and confirms your crew. The deposit amount is 20% of your estimated move total, rounded up to the nearest dollar.

Your deposit is applied to your final balance

The deposit is not an additional charge. It is credited toward your total moving cost. Nothing is added on top of it.

Rescheduling. Need to change your move date? Text us at 540-300-1414 at least 24 hours before your scheduled move and we will get you rescheduled at no charge.

Cancellations. If you decide not to move forward after booking, your deposit is fully refunded. Just let us know before your scheduled move date.

No-shows. If we arrive on move day and cannot proceed due to customer unavailability without prior notice, your deposit may be forfeited. If something came up, text us right away at (540) 300-1414 and we will do our best to work with you.

Scope of your guaranteed price. Your flat rate covers what is submitted in your online moving plan. Any rooms, items, or spaces not included in your booking details are not guaranteed under the original price and may be subject to adjustment.

Section 3

Damage & Liability

VYV is a licensed and insured moving company operating in Virginia. We take care of your belongings. In the event of damage, our liability is governed by the following terms.

Released value protection (included at no charge). Unless you arrange separate insurance, our liability for damaged or lost items is limited to 60 cents per pound per article, which is the standard released value rate established under federal and Virginia moving regulations. For example, a 50-pound item would carry a maximum liability of $30.00 under this coverage, regardless of its actual value.

Released value may not cover your belongings at full replacement cost

If you own high-value items such as antiques, artwork, electronics, or jewelry, we strongly recommend purchasing a separate moving insurance policy or confirming coverage under your homeowners or renters insurance before moving day.

Items excluded from coverage. VYV is not liable for damage to items that were:

  • Packed by the customer (not professionally packed by our crew)
  • Already damaged or in poor condition before the move
  • Not included in your submitted moving plan inventory
  • Items of extraordinary value not disclosed at the time of booking

Reporting damage. Any damage must be noted on the moving day paperwork before the crew departs. Claims reported after the crew leaves and after paperwork is signed may not be accepted. If you notice damage during the move, say something immediately.

VYV is not liable for delays caused by weather, road conditions, acts of third parties, or circumstances outside our reasonable control.

Section 4

Privacy Policy

Validate Your Vision is committed to protecting your personal information. This section explains what we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

What we collect. When you use our online Moving Blueprint Builder or contact us, we may collect:

  • Name, email address, and phone number
  • Home address and destination address
  • Move details (home size, items, dates, services requested)
  • Device and browser information collected automatically by analytics tools

How we use it. We use your information only to:

  • Respond to your estimate request and confirm your move details
  • Communicate with you about your move
  • Improve our website and services through aggregated analytics data

Analytics. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our website. This tool collects anonymized data such as pages visited, time on site, and general location. It does not collect your name or contact details. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Form submissions. Our estimate form is processed by Formspree. Your submission data is transmitted securely and stored on Formspree's servers. We do not sell or share your personal information with any third party for marketing purposes.

Text messaging. Our phone number is text-only. When you text us, standard message and data rates may apply depending on your carrier plan. We do not enroll you in any automated messaging program without your explicit consent.

Data retention. We retain your information only as long as necessary to fulfill your move and comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your data at any time by texting or emailing us.

Your rights. You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data at any time by contacting us at hello@validateyourvision.com.

Cookies. Our site uses cookies for analytics and to remember your preferences. You can disable cookies in your browser settings, though some site features may not function as expected.
