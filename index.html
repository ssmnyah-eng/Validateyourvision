FAQ

Questions We Hear
All the Time

How far in advance should I book?
+
We recommend booking at least 2–4 weeks in advance, especially for summer months (May–September) when demand is highest. That said, we often accommodate last-minute moves — just call us and we'll do our best.
Is my binding quote really guaranteed?
+
Yes, absolutely. A binding quote means the price is locked in based on your inventory and distance. The only time costs change is if you add significant items or services after the quote is finalized — and we'll always discuss that with you first.
What happens if something gets damaged?
+
We carry full value protection insurance on every move. In the very rare event of damage, we handle your claim quickly and fairly — no long waits, no runaround. Our 99% damage-free rate speaks to our care, but our insurance coverage means you're protected regardless.
Do you move specialty items like pianos or safes?
+
Yes! We have specialist crews and equipment for pianos, gun safes, pool tables, large artwork, antiques, and other high-value or oversized items. Just let us know in advance so we can bring the right gear and expertise.
Do I need to be home during the move?
+
We recommend being present at both pickup and delivery to walk through the inventory with us. If you absolutely cannot be there, we can work with a trusted representative — just let your coordinator know in advance.
What should I do with plants and perishables?
+
We're happy to transport plants for local moves. For long-distance moves, we advise transporting plants personally in your vehicle. Perishable food should be consumed or donated before moving day — we can remind you of this in our pre-move checklist.
