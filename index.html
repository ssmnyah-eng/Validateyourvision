Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

How much does it cost to hire movers in Fredericksburg, VA?

Moving costs depend on a lot of factors: your home size, how furnished it is, stairs, how far the truck needs to park, specialty items, drive distance, whether you need packing, and how long the job takes. That is why we built our personalized moving plan. It walks through all of those details and generates a real estimate range based on your specific situation, not a generic number based on bedroom count. Start there and you will have a much clearer picture before we ever talk.

Do you do in-home walkthroughs before moving day?

Yes, for larger, more complex, or full-service moves, we schedule a walkthrough consultation to confirm your inventory, identify specialty items, evaluate access, and finalize your move plan. Smaller moves often start and finish with our online moving plan tool. Either way, we review everything with you before anything is confirmed and no crew shows up without a clear plan already in place.

How is your estimate calculated?

Your estimate is based on the actual details of your move, including crew size needed, estimated hours, stair and access factors, drive distance, specialty items, and whether packing is included. The final cost comes down to how long the job takes. Because we gather real information upfront, our estimates are detailed and rarely far off. You will receive a low-to-high range so you know what to plan for.

Can you handle both packing and moving?

Yes. Our All-Inclusive Package covers packing, moving, and unpacking from start to finish. You get one crew, one plan, and a bundled rate for doing everything together. Packing can also be added as a standalone service if you just need help with that part.

How far in advance should I book movers?

We recommend booking at least 2 to 4 weeks in advance, especially for weekend moves or end-of-month dates when demand is highest. For last-minute moves, text us at 540-300-1414 and we will do our best to accommodate you.

What areas do you serve?

We serve a roughly 90-mile radius from Fredericksburg, VA. That includes Stafford, Spotsylvania, Woodbridge, Manassas, Alexandria, Arlington, Fairfax, Reston, Ashburn, Leesburg, Richmond, Glen Allen, Midlothian, Charlottesville, Winchester, and dozens of communities in between. If you are not sure whether we cover your area, text us and we will confirm quickly.

Do you offer unpacking and organizing services?

Yes. We offer unpacking, standard organizing, and White Glove Organizing, where we place, arrange, and set up your entire home until it feels lived-in from the moment we leave. These can be added to any move or booked as standalone services.

Do you handle long distance moves from Fredericksburg, VA?

Yes. We handle long-distance relocations with the same structured planning process, including a detailed moving plan built around your actual route and home. Text us at 540-300-1414 to talk through your long-distance move and what it would involve.

How do I get a moving estimate?

Complete our personalized moving plan. It takes a few minutes and covers your home, move details, and any services you need. Once submitted, we will call you to confirm your information and schedule your in-home moving consultation. That visit is where we walk your space, finalize the details, and build your complete move plan.
