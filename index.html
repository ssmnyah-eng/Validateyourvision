More Questions
Do I need 3 movers?⌵

Most moving companies send 2 movers at a lower hourly rate. VYV offers both 2 and 3-person crews. A 3-person crew works about 40% faster, which makes the total cost lower even if the hourly rate is higher.

Cost Comparison Example

2 Movers:
Estimated hours: 6.0 hrs  ·  Rate: $165/hr  ·  Total: $990

3 Movers:
Estimated hours: 3.6 hrs  ·  Rate: $199/hr  ·  Total: $716

Savings: ~$274 — 3 movers costs about 28% less for a typical 3BR move.

Why do customers save more with 3 movers?⌵
Three movers finish moves significantly faster than two. When you factor in the reduced hours, the total bill is often lower despite the higher hourly rate. We recommend 3 movers for any 2-bedroom home or larger.
Can you do same day or next day moves?⌵
Yes, when availability allows. Text us at (540) 300-1414 and we'll let you know what's open. Last-minute moves are subject to crew availability.
How much do movers charge per hour?⌵
VYV charges $165/hr for 2 movers and $199/hr for 3 movers, Tuesday through Thursday. Friday rates start at $180/hr. Weekend rates are $199/hr for 2 movers and $235/hr for 3 movers. All rates have a 2-hour minimum. A flat truck fee based on drive time is added separately.
How far in advance should I book?⌵
We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance, especially for weekend moves. Summer and end-of-month dates fill up fast. You can reserve your date with just a $50 priority deposit to hold your walkthrough slot.
How do I schedule, reschedule, or cancel my move?⌵
Book online through our move form or text us at (540) 300-1414. Rescheduling is free before your crew is dispatched. Simply text us and we'll find a new date that works.
