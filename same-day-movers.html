Our Same Day Moving Process

Pre-Move Communication

Same day moves can be unpredictable, but we make them feel effortless. Our team jumps into action right away, gathering the info we need and keeping you in the loop from start to finish.

Here's how our same day moving process works:

  1. Text the number for your area. Please have the move from and move to addresses ready and the square footage of your residence ready.
  2. Our team will start a quote and confirm availability for your area.
  3. If there is early AM availability, we'll advise of arrival time.
  4. If the same day is available for a flex or PM move, we'll advise of arrival window after your mover completes their previous job.
  5. If the timing works for you, we will book you and collect a 20% deposit based on your estimated move total (which is applied to your total bill).
  6. A confirmation of the move will be sent to the contact info you provide.
  7. For an apartment move, we will secure a certificate of insurance for your apartment manager.
  8. Finally, you'll receive communication directly from your crew when they are on the way.

Crew Arrival and Pre-Move Walkthrough

Our reliable crew arrives promptly, dressed in uniform, driving a clean VYV Moving truck equipped with all the necessary tools and supplies to handle your move efficiently. Upon arrival, our crew lead will conduct a thorough walkthrough of your home with you to:

  • Introduce the team assigned to your job
  • Identify the nearest stairway or elevator for smooth access
  • Confirm exactly which items are being moved
  • Note any specific order for moving rooms or belongings
  • Inspect items for any pre-existing damage and record details
  • Identify items requiring waiver signatures (such as washer/dryer disconnections or moving TVs)
  • Determine what needs to be disassembled and later reassembled during the move
  • Review and confirm rates and the estimate, followed by signing the contract

We understand that sometimes you may have a last minute moving request, and we're here to help — just text us anytime to get started.

Move Preparation And Protection

After the crew leader completes the initial walkthrough, the team begins preparing your building, apartment, and belongings to ensure a smooth and damage-free move. This careful preparation is especially important for a fast, same day relocation. To protect your home and items, we use:

  • Protective runners to shield your floors from scratches and scuffs
  • Shrink wrap to guard furniture fabrics against dirt and tears
  • Moving blankets to cover hard surfaces and prevent items from rubbing or shifting during transit
  • Specialized tools to safely disassemble and reassemble furniture or other bulky items

We pride ourselves on transparent pricing with no hidden fees, so you can trust the process is efficient.

Efficient Truck Loading

This is where our expertise truly stands out. You'll notice the care, speed, and teamwork our movers bring to every step of your move. Usually, one mover focuses on loading the truck itself, while the others efficiently carry your belongings from your home to the truck. This approach allows us to:

  • Load the truck strategically to maximize space, protecting your valuable items and minimizing the chance of damage. Proper packing also reduces the likelihood of needing a second trip to your new destination.
  • Have a mover stationed inside the truck to maintain security and ensure nothing is misplaced or stolen during transit.

Final Walk-Through

After we've carefully loaded all your stuff, our team will do a final walk-through with you to ensure:

  • Every item you want to move is properly accounted for
  • All our tools and equipment are back on the truck
  • We're fully prepared and ready to head to your new location

This step helps us guarantee a smooth process and gives our clients peace of mind before we hit the road.

Destination Walk Through

Once we arrive at your destination, our crew leader will walk through the space with you to decide exactly where your belongings should be placed and arranged. Then, our team will carefully:

  • Protect the floors to keep everything safe
  • Unload your items according to your directions
  • Reassemble any furniture as needed
  • Remove all shrink wrap and moving materials used during the move

Final Walkthrough and Completion

Once all your belongings are safely unloaded, our team will walk through your new space with you to ensure everything is exactly where it should be. During this final step, we make sure:

  • Items are placed in the correct rooms according to your instructions
  • All packing materials and debris are cleaned up and removed
  • Any furniture disassembled during the move is fully reassembled
  • Elevators or building equipment we've used are left clean and ready for others
  • You're completely satisfied with the results

Whether this is a carefully planned move or a last minute relocation, our goal is to leave you stress-free and settled in.

At the end of the walkthrough, the crew leader will stop the clock and review the total hours worked. You'll receive a clear, final bill, and once payment is completed, we'll leave you to enjoy your new space.

With a same day moving company like ours, moving doesn't have to be a hassle — it's just one smooth step at a time.

Be Ready

Preparing for a Last Minute Move

When time is short, a little preparation goes a long way. Start by identifying your highest-priority items and boxing them up first. Loose items take far longer to load than boxed ones, so even partial packing makes a big difference in how quickly your crew can work.

Pack essentials first
Documents, medications, chargers, and anything you'll need the first night.
Clear a path
Make sure hallways and doorways are clear so your crew can move quickly and safely.
Secure parking
Reserve a spot close to the entrance at both your origin and destination for the moving truck.
Label boxes by room
Even a quick marker label helps your crew place boxes in the right rooms at your new place.

Our flexible scheduling means we can work around your timeline. Once you're booked, your dedicated crew handles the heavy lifting--literally. The value of professional movers on a last-minute timeline isn't just speed; it's protecting your belongings and your sanity.
