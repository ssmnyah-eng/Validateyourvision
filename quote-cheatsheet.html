STEP 3 -- Mileage Fee

  • Pull exact miles on Google Maps using both addresses
  • Miles × $3/mile (adjust to your rate)

STEP 4 -- Buffer Rule (NEVER Skip This)

⚠️ Add 20% to your total labor hours
⚠️ Always quote the MIDPOINT -- never the low end
⚠️ Set a 3-hour MINIMUM no matter what

STEP 5 -- Overage Protection

Tell every client:

"Quoted hours are the minimum. Anything over bills in 30-minute increments."

✅ Put this in writing before every job.

📌 Example Quote

Job: 3BR home · 1 flight of stairs · piano · 15 miles away

  • Base hours: 8 hrs
  • + Stairs: 0.75 hrs
  • + Piano: 2 hrs
  • = 10.75 hrs → round to 11 hrs
  • + 20% buffer → quote 13 hours minimum
  • Mileage: 15 × $3 = $45

Total Quote: (13 × your hourly rate) + $45

Quick Reminders

  • Walk every room during the in-home walkthrough -- never quote blind
  • Ask about items NOT in plain sight (attic, garage, shed, storage unit)
  • Confirm if client is packing themselves or needs you to pack
  • Always confirm elevator access or loading dock at destination
  • If it feels like more than estimated -- it is. Trust your gut and add time.
