# VYV Blog Topics — Master List
# Built from Notion strategy research: audience personas, pain points, content pillars, trigger phrases

---

## How to Use This List

Every topic below maps to:
- A **content pillar** (why we're writing it)
- A **persona** (who it's for)
- A **hook angle** (the emotional entry point based on where they are in the journey)
- A **SEO intent** (what they're typing into Google)

The strategy says content should meet customers at Stage 2 (Anxiety) and Stage 3 (Overwhelm).
That is the emotional state of almost every topic below. The reader is not excited yet — they're scared.
Speak to that first. Show the relief second.
