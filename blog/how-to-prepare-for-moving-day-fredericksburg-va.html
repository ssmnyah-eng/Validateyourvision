Moving Tips

How to Prepare for Moving Day in Fredericksburg, VA

VYV Team · June 2025 · 5 min read
← Back to all posts

Most moving day stress comes from things that could have been handled the week before. Whether you are moving across Fredericksburg or relocating to the Northern Virginia suburbs, a little prep turns a chaotic morning into a smooth, on-schedule move.

The Week Before Your Move

The week before is your window to handle everything that cannot be done in a rush. Use it.

Confirm your moving crew

If you are working with VYV, you will already have a move plan and a confirmed date. Touch base to verify the start time, crew size, and any last-minute changes to your inventory. If you added a piano or a hot tub since the in-home consultation, let your mover know now, not moving morning.

Finish packing room by room

Professional movers work fastest when boxes are sealed, labeled, and staged near the door. Aim to be fully packed at least two days before your move date. Leave out only what you need to get through the next few nights.

  • Label every box with the room it goes to in your new home, not where it came from
  • Mark fragile boxes on the top and at least one side
  • Keep a separate "open first" box with essentials: phone charger, toiletries, a change of clothes, snacks, coffee maker
  • Disassemble furniture you know needs to come apart, like bed frames and large desks
Pro tip from VYV Use color-coded tape by room. One roll of blue painter's tape per room costs about two dollars and saves your crew from reading every box label. Stick a matching color swatch on each doorframe in the new house.

Handle logistics

  • Reserve a parking spot or loading zone at both addresses if needed
  • Notify your building or HOA if they require an elevator reservation or move-in window
  • Change your address with USPS, your bank, your employer, and any subscriptions
  • Transfer or cancel utilities so nothing is left running at the old place

The Night Before Your Move

The night before sets the tone for everything that follows. Keep it simple.

  • Do a final walkthrough of every room, closet, cabinet, and drawer
  • Check the attic, garage, and outdoor storage
  • Charge your phone and make sure your moving day contact numbers are saved
  • Set out your "open first" box somewhere obvious so it does not get buried in the truck
  • Get to bed at a reasonable hour. Fatigue is the most underrated moving day problem.
Do not forget Defrost your refrigerator the night before if it is moving with you. A wet, leaking fridge on moving morning is a real issue and something movers encounter constantly.

Moving Day Morning

When your crew arrives, they need to be able to work. Here is how to make that happen.

  1. Be awake and ready 15 minutes before your scheduled start time
  2. Clear a path from the main entrance to where boxes and furniture are staged
  3. Point out anything fragile, high-value, or that requires special handling right away
  4. Keep children and pets in a room away from the work area for their safety and your crew's focus
  5. Have water or drinks available for the crew, especially in summer heat
  6. Do a final walkthrough of the old home before the truck leaves

What to keep with you, not in the truck

  • Valuables: jewelry, important documents, medication
  • Anything irreplaceable
  • Your "open first" box
  • Laptop and phone chargers
  • Cash or payment method for the final invoice
