Pricing & Fees

How much do movers cost in Fredericksburg, VA?

VYV charges $165/hr for 2 movers and $199/hr for 3 movers on weekdays (Tuesday–Thursday). Friday rates start at $180/hr. Weekend rates are $199/hr for 2 movers and $235/hr for 3 movers. All rates include a 2-hour minimum.

A flat truck fee (based on drive time to your location) is added separately. For most moves within 50 miles, the truck fee is $99.

Are there any hidden fees?
No. You pay your hourly rate for time worked, plus the flat truck fee. No fuel surcharges. No stair fees. No heavy-item fees. No add-ons at the end of the job that you didn't agree to upfront.
Is 3 movers actually cheaper than 2?

Usually, yes — especially for 2-bedroom homes or larger. Three movers work about 40% faster than two, which means fewer billable hours. Here's a real comparison:

Same 3-Hour Move Example

2 Movers — 3.5 hrs × $165 + $99 truck = $677

3 Movers — 2 hrs × $199 + $99 truck = $497

3 movers saves you ~$180 on a typical 2BR move.

How does the deposit work?
A $50 priority deposit reserves your date and walkthrough slot. It's applied directly to your balance on move day — it's not an extra charge. You pay the remaining balance after the move is complete.
When does billing start?
Billing starts when you check in your crew using your phone — not when they leave the warehouse. You control the clock. If your crew shows up and you're not ready, the meter doesn't run until you sign them in.

Booking & Scheduling

How far in advance should I book?
We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance, especially for weekend and end-of-month dates. Summer months book out fast. A $50 deposit locks in your date — you can always adjust later if your plans change.
Can you do same-day or next-day moves?
Yes, when crew availability allows. Text us at (540) 300-1414 and we'll check what's open. Last-minute moves are subject to crew availability — the sooner you reach out, the better.
How do I reschedule my move?
Just text us at (540) 300-1414 before your crew is dispatched. Rescheduling is free and we'll find a new date that works. We don't penalize you for life happening.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made at least 48 hours before your move are eligible for a deposit refund. Cancellations inside 48 hours forfeit the deposit. If something comes up, always text us first — we'd rather work with you than lose the booking.
How do I book my move?
Book entirely online at validateyourvision.com/book — pick your crew size, choose a date, and pay your $50 deposit to lock it in. No phone calls, no sales process. The whole thing takes under 2 minutes.

Move Day

What happens when the crew arrives?
Your crew will introduce themselves, do a quick walkthrough of what's moving, and then wait for you to check them in on your phone. The clock starts when you sign them in — not before. They'll wrap furniture, load the truck efficiently, and keep you updated throughout the job.
How should I prepare for moving day?

A few things that help your crew work faster (and keep your bill lower):

  • Have boxes packed, sealed, and labeled before the crew arrives
  • Disassemble bed frames and large furniture in advance if you can
  • Clear a path to the door and reserve a parking spot for the truck
  • Set aside anything that's NOT moving so there's no confusion
  • Have your phone charged — you'll need it to check in your crew
Do the movers disassemble and reassemble furniture?
Yes. Basic disassembly and reassembly (bed frames, desks, dining tables) is included. If you have complex or specialty furniture, mention it when you book so we can plan appropriately.
How does payment work at the end of the move?
When the job is complete, your crew lead will confirm the total hours worked. Your $50 deposit is deducted and you pay the remaining balance. We accept all major credit cards, debit cards, and Zelle. No cash required.

Packing & Supplies

Do you provide boxes and packing materials?
We bring moving blankets, straps, and shrink wrap for all furniture protection. Boxes and packing tape can be arranged — just mention it when you book and we'll include them in your plan.
Should I pack before the movers arrive?
Yes, whenever possible. Arriving to find packed and sealed boxes lets the crew load immediately — which saves time and reduces your bill. If you need help packing, we offer full packing services. Just add it to your booking.
How do you handle fragile or high-value items?
All furniture is wrapped in moving blankets before loading. For fragile items like TVs, mirrors, and artwork, we use specialty wrap and pad them carefully. If you have particularly high-value items, let us know in advance so we can bring the right materials.
What items can't movers transport?
We can't transport hazardous materials (propane tanks, paint cans, chemicals), perishable food, live plants, or pets. For everything else, ask us — most specialty items just need a heads up so we can prepare.
