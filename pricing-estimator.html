Common Questions & Honest Answers
Things People Wonder Before Booking VYV
"Your price seems higher than some other quotes I got."
▼

We hear this sometimes. Here's what we've learned: lower quotes often come from companies that haven't actually seen your home, use day laborers with no background checks, or add fees on moving day that weren't in the original quote.

Our estimate is built from real information, your actual home, your actual items, your actual route. The low-to-high range you see here is designed to reflect what you'll actually pay, not just get you to sign a contract.

The most expensive move is one that goes wrong. Broken furniture, a crew that doesn't show up, a bill that doubled on moving day, that's a real cost people don't think about when chasing the lowest number.
"Can I just rent a truck and do it myself to save money?"
▼

Absolutely, for small moves with light furniture and a flexible schedule, it can make sense. But most families underestimate the cost: truck rental, fuel, moving blankets, dollies, boxes, the physical toll, the time, and the risk of injury or damage without the right equipment and technique.

A professional crew also moves 2–3x faster than most families working on their own. When you add up time off work, two days of exhaustion, and the risk of something going wrong, the math often looks different than it did at the start.

Ask yourself what your time and energy are worth on one of the most stressful days of the year. That's part of the equation too.
"Will the price change on moving day?"
▼

Your estimate is based on how long the job takes, which depends on what's being moved and the conditions on moving day. Because we gather detailed information upfront, including stairs, carry distance, specialty items, and your home size, our estimates are accurate and rarely far off.

The range you see here is intentional. We give you a low and high so you can budget with confidence, not a single number that may not reflect reality.

We don't show up and surprise you. If anything in your move changes before moving day, we adjust your plan together, not after the truck is loaded.
"I've had bad experiences with movers before."
▼

We know. The moving industry has a reputation problem, and a lot of it is earned. Late arrivals, broken items, charges that appeared out of nowhere, crews that didn't care, these are real experiences people have had.

VYV was built specifically in response to that. We do in-person walkthroughs before quoting. We communicate before, during, and after. We treat your belongings the way we'd want ours treated. And we don't cut corners with who we hire.

"Professional, prompt, took great care of our stuff. I fully and whole-heartedly recommend them." (Meagan V., Northern Virginia)
"Do I need to be home the entire time?"
▼

We recommend being present for the initial walkthrough at the start of the move and at the final delivery to confirm everything arrived and is placed correctly. During the move itself, you don't have to hover, but having someone available to answer questions and make decisions keeps things moving smoothly.

Kids and pets do best when they're in one room away from the work area, or ideally, with a sitter for the day. It makes the move faster and less stressful for everyone.

"What if something gets damaged?"
▼

We handle your belongings with care from the moment we touch them. But if something does happen, we deal with it directly, no corporate runaround, no waiting months for a claims department to respond.

During your consultation, we walk through how claims work so you know the process before moving day. We'd rather talk about it upfront than have you discover it after something goes wrong.

We're a locally-owned company. Our reputation is personal. When something goes wrong, it matters to us the same way it matters to you.
"How do I know you'll actually show up?"
▼

This is a fair question given how many people have been burned by no-shows. Our crew members are direct employees, not day laborers pulled from a list the night before. When you book a date with VYV, that date belongs to you.

If anything changes on our end, you hear from us first, not on moving day morning. We communicate proactively because we know what's at stake when someone is counting on us to show up.
